## The idea

Make gravity fun to play with in a few seconds. Aim, launch, watch the path bend, correct.

## What I did

- A minimal, tested physics engine: softened attraction, semi-implicit Euler integration, sub-steps for close fly-bys.
- Five hand-made levels, each checked by a test that tries thousands of shots: solvable, but not trivial.
- Aim with the mouse or the keyboard (arrows, Space), a predicted path, and status announced to screen readers.
- Loaded only on first play: zero bytes for those who do not play.

## Outcome

A few-minute game built into the site, with a best score per level and stars based on attempts.
